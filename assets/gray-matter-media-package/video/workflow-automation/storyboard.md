# CUT A — "The Story" (~45 seconds)

- **Style:** Flat vector motion graphics — clean, bold, blue-collar straightforward. No tech-bro neon, no corporate stock.
- **Voice:** Plain-spoken contractor. Calm, direct, "I've seen this a hundred times."
- **Music:** Upbeat but grounded — light acoustic/percussive bed, not corporate EDM. Duck under VO.
- **Captions:** Burn in captions — most Facebook views are muted.

**Build path:** Generate each scene as a short clip in Higgsfield using the visual prompt, then assemble in order and lay the voiceover + music bed over the top. Keep one consistent style prefix across all scene prompts (e.g. "flat vector motion graphic, dark slate background, bold sans-serif type, subtle tech-pattern texture").

---

## A1 — The feeling (0:00–0:05)

**Visual (Higgsfield prompt):** Flat vector motion graphic, dark slate background. A smartphone at center with message notification bubbles stacking up faster and faster around it. Sticky notes and a paper calendar swirl in the background. Slight camera push-in. Mood: overwhelming but relatable, not bleak.

**On-screen text:** "Still copying the same info into three different places?"

**Voiceover:** "You know the feeling. Orders living in your DMs. Follow-ups living in your head."

**Audio/transition:** Phone buzz SFX under VO. Quick wipe into A2.

---

## A2 — Five steps, all yours (0:05–0:12)

**Visual (Higgsfield prompt):** Flat vector motion graphic, same style. A horizontal conveyor of five task cards lighting up one by one: "Copy the info" → "Send the email" → "Update the spreadsheet" → "Remember to follow up." Each card gets stamped with a bold red "YOU" as it lights. Camera tracks left to right with the conveyor.

**On-screen text:** "Before — five steps, all yours."

**Voiceover:** "A customer fills out a form. Then you copy it. You email it. You update the spreadsheet. You try to remember to follow up. Five steps — all yours."

**Audio/transition:** Rhythmic tick SFX as each card stamps. Hard cut to A3.

---

## A3 — Something slips (0:12–0:17)

**Visual (Higgsfield prompt):** Flat vector motion graphic, same style. A clock face spinning fast. One task card fades and falls off the conveyor into darkness. A message bubble pops up reading "Is my order ready?" — asked again. Desaturated, slightly slower motion.

**On-screen text:** "Follow-up only happens… when you remember."

**Voiceover:** "Something slips. A quote never gets answered. An order gets buried."

**Audio/transition:** Music dips. A soft "thud" as the card falls. Fade to calm.

---

## A4 — The turn (0:17–0:22)

**Visual (Higgsfield prompt):** Flat vector motion graphic, same style. The chaos freezes. A calm workman's hand (rolled sleeve, no face) reaches in and straightens the conveyor with one motion — like a contractor sizing up a job. Gray Matter logo mark fades in clean. Everything goes still and orderly.

**On-screen text:** "It's never as bad as it feels."

**Voiceover:** "Here's the thing: I've seen this a hundred times. It's never as bad as it feels."

**Audio/transition:** Music lifts. Satisfying "click" as the conveyor straightens.

---

## A5 — One step, automatic (0:22–0:30)

**Visual (Higgsfield prompt):** Flat vector motion graphic, same style. Same customer form from A2 — but now a single tap sends a glowing pulse down an automated line: a confirmation sends (envelope flies), records update (checklist checks itself), follow-up schedules (calendar fills). Green checkmarks pop at each step. Smooth, satisfying flow — no hands touching anything.

**On-screen text:** "After — one step, and it's theirs."

**Voiceover:** "A little automation here, a little there. Confirmations send themselves. Records update themselves. Follow-up gets scheduled, not remembered."

**Audio/transition:** Soft chime on each checkmark. Flow carries into A6.

# CUT B — "The Sizzle" (~18 seconds)

- **Style:** Cinematic SaaS / Apple-style product promo. Fluid motion, vibrant color, premium depth — think Apple keynote sizzle, not explainer cartoon. No voiceover; kinetic typography + music carry it.
- **Arc:** Chaos → order. Opens in beautiful overwhelm, resolves into a clean running system.
- **Music:** Modern, uplifting electronic with a build and a clean resolve. No lyrics.

**Build path:** Generate each scene as a short clip in Higgsfield using the visual prompt, then assemble in order and lay the music bed + SFX over the top. Lock one style prefix and reuse it verbatim in every prompt so the clips cut together (e.g. "cinematic 3D motion graphic, dark premium background, vibrant gradient light trails, fluid slow camera drift, ultra clean, Apple keynote style").

**Logo:** End card uses the actual logo pulled from graymatterdigitalsolutions.com (site header). If the header mark is a text wordmark, recreate it in the site's matching type and weight — do not substitute a generic icon.

---

## B1 — Chaos (0:00–0:06)

**Visual (Higgsfield prompt):** Cinematic 3D motion graphic, dark premium background, Apple keynote style. A vibrant vortex of message bubbles, notification badges, browser tabs, and sticky notes swirling in slow motion around the camera — colorful, fluid, overwhelming but beautiful. Shallow depth of field, light trails, gentle camera drift through the chaos.

**On-screen text (kinetic type, builds with the swirl):** "Drowning in DMs?"

**Audio:** Music starts low and busy. Soft notification pings layering up.

**Transition:** The vortex begins to slow — everything hangs for a beat, then B2 snaps it into order with a single fluid morph (no hard cut).

---

## B2 — Chaos becomes order (0:06–0:13)

**Visual (Higgsfield prompt):** Cinematic 3D motion graphic, same style. The swirling chaos from B1 freezes mid-air, then every element snaps magnetically into a clean glowing flow line: a form → a confirmation → a checklist checking itself → a calendar filling. Vibrant gradient pulses travel down the line like current. Camera glides smoothly along the flow. Everything moves with weightless precision.

**On-screen text (kinetic type, timed to the pulses):**
"Manual" — crosses out, morphs into — "Automatic."

**Audio:** Music builds — a rising tone as the line forms, then a satisfying chime on each completed step.

**Transition:** The flow line contracts gracefully toward center screen, dissolving into the logo reveal.
