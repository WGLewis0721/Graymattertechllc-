# Promo 2: Gray Matter, a TechOps Studio (plan)

Brand introduction. Promo 1 sold one service. This one says what Gray Matter *is*, and introduces the label "TechOps studio" with a clear meaning attached.

- **Format:** square 1080x1080 and vertical 1080x1920, about 22.8s, built on the promo 1 template so the two read as one series
- **Look:** the same locked style prefix, navy/cobalt/mint palette, intro card, dark end card, 100 BPM beat grid
- **Status:** built. See [README.md](README.md) for the final files. Changes from this plan: the music is "Hip Hop - Upbeat" by MusicForPeople at 90 BPM (scenes are 6 and 9 beats, 24.0s in total), the clips are Seedance 2.5 anchored on shared frames, and each word underlines its icon.

---

## 1. The message

**Source copy (yours):**
> a TechOps studio for businesses that know something needs to work better, but don't necessarily know what the fix looks like yet.
>
> Websites. Systems. Automation. Operations. Technology.

**What "TechOps" means, in one sentence the video and post can both carry:**
> TechOps is the technology side of how your business runs: the website customers see, the systems and automations behind it, and the day-to-day operations and tech that keep it all working. A studio handles all of it in one place, start to finish.

**The five words, mapped to services the site already sells** (so every claim has a page behind it):

| Word | What it means for the owner | Site services |
|---|---|---|
| Websites | A professional place for customers to find you, book, and pay | Website Solutions, Customer Action System |
| Systems | What's in your head, written down so others can run it | Process & Documentation Systems |
| Automation | Follow-ups, confirmations and order updates that happen without you | Workflow Automation, AI Command Center |
| Operations | Work from anywhere, costs that make sense, one place to call | Work From Anywhere, Technology Cost Cleanup, Technology Partner |
| Technology | Accounts, devices and files that are secure and backed up | Business Security, Backup & Recovery, Technology Checkup |

**The story in three beats:** you can feel something isn't working → you don't need to know the fix → here's everything a TechOps studio covers.

## 2. Timeline (100 BPM, 0.6s per beat)

| Time | Scene | Picture | On-screen text (one line) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-3.6 | Intro card | Brain mark on the dark card, blue rule draws out | kicker **MEET GRAY MATTER**, title **Your TechOps Studio** |
| 3.6-7.2 | 1. The feeling | Clip 1: a tangled knot of glowing threads, twisting, a few flickering | **It should work better.** |
| 7.2-10.8 | 2. The turn | Clip 2: the knot is drawn apart into five clean parallel lanes of light | **Don't know the fix yet?** swaps to **That's our job.** |
| 10.8-16.8 | 3. What TechOps means | Clip 3 (6s): the five lanes each feed a module that lights up in turn, left to right | small kicker **TECHOPS**, then one big word per module, 2 beats each: **Websites. Systems. Automation. Operations. Technology.** |
| 16.8-22.8 | End card | Lanes contract to a point of light, logo resolves out of it | Logo, then **WEBSITES · SYSTEMS · AUTOMATION · OPERATIONS · TECHNOLOGY** (small, tracked), tagline, website and phone |

Why this order: the first 7 seconds speak to the viewer's problem, not to Gray Matter. The label "TechOps" lands only after the viewer has a reason to care, and then the five words define it on the spot.

All the text fits on one line at title size (measured): "It should work better." 875px, "Don't know the fix yet?" 895px at 80px, "That's our job." 586px, "Your TechOps Studio" 856px.

## 3. Clip prompts

Every prompt starts with the locked style prefix from promo 1, word for word:

> cinematic 3D motion graphic, deep navy background #152238, cobalt blue #2F64D6 gradient light trails, soft mint and gold accents, fluid slow camera drift, premium depth of field, ultra clean, Apple keynote style, square 1:1 framing, no readable text, no letters, no logos, UI elements show abstract bars instead of words

**Clip 1, the feeling (4s):**
[prefix]. A tangled knot of glowing threads floats in the center of the frame, slowly twisting in on itself. A few threads flicker and dim as if a connection keeps dropping. The camera drifts slowly around it. Mood: something is not quite working, uneasy but beautiful. In the final second the knot holds still.

**Clip 2, the turn (4s):**
[prefix]. Opening on a tangled knot of glowing threads, still in mid-air. The threads loosen and are drawn apart smoothly, untangling into five clean, evenly spaced parallel lanes of light that run left to right across the frame. Cobalt pulses begin to travel along each lane. Calm, precise, satisfying.

**Clip 3, what TechOps means (6s):**
[prefix]. Five evenly spaced horizontal lanes of light run left to right. At the end of each lane sits a small glowing glass module, and they light up one at a time from top to bottom, about one second apart: a browser window, a stack of layered panels, a set of interlocking gears, a dashboard dial, a shield with a cloud. Pulses flow along each lane as its module lights. Camera glides slowly. Ends with all five lit, steady.

The module icons are abstract and wordless. The words come from editing, timed to the moment each module lights up (I'll measure those frames like I did for the toggle in promo 1).
