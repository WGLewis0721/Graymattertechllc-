# "Then vs. Now" — AI Sports Ad Series

Status: planning. Nothing here has been generated or published yet.

## Concept
Inspired by Gatorade's "23 vs. 39" (2003): an athlete plays one-on-one against
their younger self. We make the same format with **original fictional
athletes** (see `RIGHTS_AND_RISK.md` for why), cinematic ad-style, 30–45 sec,
vertical + horizontal cuts.

Three-episode launch slate (placeholders — rename freely):
1. **"Prospect vs. Prime"** — a veteran guard vs. his rookie self, playground court.
2. **"Sharpshooter"** — a long-range specialist vs. his college-gym self, a shot-for-shot duel.
3. **"The Big Man"** — a big vs. his teen self in a high-school gym; a friendly-rivalry ending.

Each episode ends on a sponsor tag card. The sponsor is either a fictional
drink brand (for the portfolio) or a real local client of Gray Matter (see
`MONETIZATION.md`).

## Pipeline
1. **Character bible** — one page per original character: age at both ends,
   build, signature moves, wardrobe, court. Reuse the approach in
   `haiku-character-content-system/` so the characters stay consistent.
2. **Script + shot list** — 6–8 shots per spot; a voiceover or a single
   original jingle line (no existing songs).
3. **Reference stills** — generate a character sheet for each version
   (young/old) first; lock them before any video.
4. **Video generation** — image-to-video from the locked stills, short clips
   (5–10 s) stitched together. Connected tools already available in this
   workspace: Higgsfield and Magnific (images, video, audio). Check pricing
   first with the cost tools — video credits add up quickly.
5. **Edit** — cut in any editor; add sound design, original music, and the
   sponsor card.
6. **Disclosure** — label as AI-generated on every platform (they require it
   for realistic synthetic content).
7. **Review gate** — a human watches every cut before posting.
