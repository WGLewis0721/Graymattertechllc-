# Client Intake Template

**Status:** CREATED · **Use:** internal, for every new prospect · **Record type:** blank template

Copy this template into a **restricted** record (CRM, private folder, or a Drive file that is not public). Do **not** fill it in this repository: this repo is public. Do not record contact details, budgets, or client data here.

Website builds also use [websites/docs/INTAKE.md](../../websites/docs/INTAKE.md) after the brief is approved. This template covers the earlier, universal discovery step.

---

## Part A — Discovery questions (about 15 minutes)

1. What does your business do, in one sentence?
2. Who are your customers, and how do they find you today?
3. What problem is costing you time, money, or missed work right now?
4. How do you handle that problem today? Which tools or people are involved?
5. What single outcome would make this project worth doing?
6. What must stay the same (tools, branding, team routines, suppliers)?
7. Which tools do you already pay for? Who owns those accounts?
8. Who makes the final decision, and who else must approve?
9. When do you need it live or finished?
10. What budget range do you have in mind? (Private. Used only to choose an engagement type; the quote sets the price.)
11. What access will we need (logins, domain, files, photos, copy), and who will provide each one?
12. Who owns your logo, photos, and written content? Do you have approved versions?
13. What would success look like 90 days after launch?
14. What is explicitly out of scope for now?
15. How would you like us to keep in touch, and how often?

## Part C — One-page client brief

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Client / project label | Use a label, not a legal name, in shared files |
| Business context | Two or three sentences from Part A, questions 1–2 |
| Problem | Question 3, in the client's words |
| Desired outcome | Question 5 and 13 |
| Current process and tools | Question 4 and 7 |
| Recommended service and engagement type | From Part B |
| Must stay the same | Question 6 |
| Access and inputs needed | Question 11 and 12, with an owner and due date for each |
| Out of scope | Question 14 |
| Decision-maker and approvers | Question 8 |
| Timeframe | Question 9 |
| Open questions for the owner | Anything the quote cannot answer yet (price, scope, terms) |
| **Next step** | One action, one owner, one date |

## Part D — Stage tracker

Record the current stage in the restricted system:

`New → Discovery → Quoted → Approved → In Delivery → Complete → Support`

- A stage changes only when its evidence exists (for example, *Approved* requires written acceptance, see the quote playbook).
- Keep the tracker out of this repo.

---

## Done when

One discovery conversation produces a clear scope decision (what, which service, which engagement type) and one next action with an owner and date.
