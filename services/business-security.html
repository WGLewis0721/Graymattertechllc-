Who Still Has Access to Your Business?

Most small business security problems aren’t dramatic. They’re an old employee who still has the password, or an email account nobody protected.

The Real Risks

It’s Usually Something Ordinary

Not hackers in hoodies. These are the gaps that actually cost small businesses money.

A former employee

Still in the email, the booking system, or the shared drive.

A shared password

One login everybody uses, and nobody can change safely.

Unprotected email

The account that can reset every other account you own.

A lost laptop

Left in a truck, with the business signed in on it.

Customer information

Names, numbers, and payment details sitting where anyone can reach them.

Business social accounts

The page you built for years, tied to one person’s login.

Gray Matter helps lock down what matters — who has access, how they get in, and what happens if a device walks off.
