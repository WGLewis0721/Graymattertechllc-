    Plain Definitions

    Prospect, Lead, Opportunity — Not the Same Thing

    Plenty of services blur these together. Here’s exactly which one you’re buying.

    Prospect

    A business that fits your target profile. It has not heard from you and has expressed no interest. This is what the research produces.

    Lead

    A business that has shown some level of interest — replied, asked a question, filled something in. Leads come from your outreach, not from a list.

    Opportunity

    Active potential business: a live bid, an open solicitation, a real conversation about scope. Some of these are public and findable; most are earned.

    What this service delivers is prospects and published opportunities. Turning a prospect into a lead is outreach, and that part is yours. Gray Matter identifies businesses and opportunities matching your target. Research does not guarantee sales or contracts.
