Barbers & Salons

Make It Easy to Find Your Shop, See Your Work, and Book

A new client shouldn’t have to scroll an Instagram grid to find out what you charge or whether you have a chair open Thursday.

Build My Shop Website See What It Looks Like
Step One

What Does Your Customer Need to Do?

This is the path a new customer takes. Every step is a place you can lose them.

  1. They search Google for a barber or salon nearby
  3. They land on your website
  5. They see cuts, services, and prices
  7. They choose a barber or stylist
  9. They book
Step Two

Where the Process Usually Breaks

These are common patterns in this kind of business — not a diagnosis of yours.

Found on social, not on the web

The profile has photos but no hours, prices, or a way to book.

Booking lives in DMs

Requests arrive at midnight and get buried under everything else.

No-shows

Nobody got a reminder, so the chair sits empty.

Clients can’t tell who does what

New clients don’t know which barber does the cut they saw.

Step Three

What Gray Matter Can Improve

Pieces you can start with, add later, or skip entirely.

Shop website

One place with services, prices, hours, and directions.

Barber & stylist profiles

Who works there, what they specialise in, and their work.

Booking links

Clients pick a person and a time without messaging you.

Gallery

The cuts and colours that sell the next appointment.

Reviews

Proof from the clients you already have.

Google presence

Show up in the map when someone searches nearby.

Follow-up automation

Reminders before, and a review request after.

Step Four

What Your Solution Might Look Like

A general transformation for this kind of business — not a specific client result.

Before
  • Prices answered one DM at a time
  • Bookings taken between clients
  • Empty chairs from no-shows
After
  • Services and prices published once
  • Clients book themselves, any hour
  • Automatic reminders before appointments

Business result: the chair fills without you working the phone.
