Step One

What Does Your Customer Need to Do?

This is the path a new customer takes. Every step is a place you can lose them.

  1. They search for the trade and their town
  3. They land on your website
  5. They look at past jobs
  7. They check you cover their area
  9. They request a quote
Step Two

Where the Process Usually Breaks

These are common patterns in this kind of business — not a diagnosis of yours.

Phone tag

Three voicemails before anyone talks about the actual job.

Photos live on a phone

Years of finished work nobody outside the truck ever sees.

Nobody knows your area

Calls from two hours away, and none from the next street.

Quotes go cold

The follow-up you meant to send on Tuesday.

Step Three

What Gray Matter Can Improve

Pieces you can start with, add later, or skip entirely.

Contractor website

Services, service area, licensing, and how to reach you.

Project gallery

Finished jobs organised by the kind of work.

Quote request form

Job details and photos up front, before you drive anywhere.

Service area

Clear boundaries, fewer wasted calls.

Follow-up automation

Quotes get chased even on a busy week.

Google presence

Show up in the map for the towns you actually serve.

Step Four

What Your Solution Might Look Like

A general transformation for this kind of business — not a specific client result.

Before
  • Quote requests by voicemail
  • Job photos stuck on a phone
  • Follow-ups depend on remembering
After
  • Quote requests with photos and details
  • A public gallery of finished jobs
  • Automatic follow-up on open quotes

Business result: you spend the day on jobs instead of on the phone.
