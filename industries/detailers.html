Detailers

Show Your Packages, Show Your Work, Let Them Book a Slot

Most detailing enquiries are the same three questions. Answer them once on a website instead of forty times a month in your messages.

Build My Detailing Website See What It Looks Like
Step One

What Does Your Customer Need to Do?

This is the path a new customer takes. Every step is a place you can lose them.

  1. They search for detailing nearby
  3. They land on your website
  5. They compare packages
  7. They pick their vehicle size
  9. They book and pay a deposit
Step Two

Where the Process Usually Breaks

These are common patterns in this kind of business — not a diagnosis of yours.

Pricing questions never stop

Every enquiry starts with “how much for an SUV?”

The work isn’t visible

Before-and-after photos are scattered across stories that expired.

Quoting eats the day

Driving out to look at a car that was never going to book.

No-shows and empty bays

A cancelled slot nobody filled.

Step Three

What Gray Matter Can Improve

Pieces you can start with, add later, or skip entirely.

Detailing website

Packages, pricing, service area, and hours in one place.

Package & pricing page

Priced by vehicle size, so the question answers itself.

Before-and-after gallery

The finished work that closes the sale.

Booking with deposits

A held slot with money behind it.

Quote request form

Photos and details up front, so you quote from your phone.

Reminders

Fewer empty bays on a Saturday.

Step Four

What Your Solution Might Look Like

A general transformation for this kind of business — not a specific client result.

Before
  • Pricing answered by message
  • Photos disappear into stories
  • Slots held on trust
After
  • Packages and prices published
  • A permanent gallery of finished work
  • Booked slots backed by a deposit

Business result: the calendar fills with customers who already know the price.
