Step One

What Does Your Customer Need to Do?

This is the path a new customer takes. Every step is a place you can lose them.

  1. They arrive by referral or search
  3. They land on your site
  5. They work out whether you handle their situation
  7. They share documents or details
  9. They book a consultation
Step Two

Where the Process Usually Breaks

These are common patterns in this kind of business — not a diagnosis of yours.

Unclear what you actually do

Service names that only make sense inside the profession.

Documents by plain email

Client records moving through unprotected inboxes.

Intake by phone

The same fifteen questions, transcribed by hand.

Enquiries go quiet

Nobody followed up, and the client went elsewhere.

Step Three

What Gray Matter Can Improve

Pieces you can start with, add later, or skip entirely.

Practice website

Who you help, what you handle, and how to start.

Plain-language service pages

The work explained the way a client would describe it.

Secure intake and upload

Documents collected somewhere appropriate.

Consultation booking

A first meeting booked without an email chain.

Security review

Who has access to client information, and why.

Backups

Client records that survive a dead machine.

Step Four

What Your Solution Might Look Like

A general transformation for this kind of business — not a specific client result.

Before
  • Services described in industry language
  • Intake by phone and email attachment
  • Enquiries followed up when there's time
After
  • Services explained the way clients ask for them
  • Structured, secure intake
  • Consultations booked and confirmed automatically

Business result: qualified clients arrive already understanding what you do.
