The Format

What Each Case Study Will Show

The same eleven things every time, so you can compare projects instead of reading marketing copy.

  1. Client and industry
  2. The problem, in their words
  3. Before — how it worked previously
  4. The goal they wanted to reach
  5. What Gray Matter built
  6. After — how it works now
  7. The business result
  8. Services used
  9. Screenshots of the finished work
  10. A testimonial, where the client gives one
  11. A way to start something similar

No metrics will be published that cannot be evidenced, and no case study will appear without the client’s permission.
