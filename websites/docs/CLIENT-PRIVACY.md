# Your site during the build: what "not live yet" actually means

While we're building your site, it is not public. Nobody can find it by searching, and nobody can open the link except you.

## The preview link is private to you

We'll send you a link to look at your site as it comes together. That link only opens after you enter your email address and a one-time code we send you on the spot. You don't need to create an account or remember a password. If someone who isn't you gets the link, they still can't get in without your email.

## Search engines can't find it

Google, Bing, and every other search engine are told not to look at your site while it's in progress. Even if someone guessed the exact web address, it will not show up in search results. This isn't a lock on the door, it's a "do not enter" sign for search engines specifically, so your site doesn't show up half-finished if someone happens to search for your business name while we're still working.

## You decide when it goes public

Your site does not go live on its own. It stays in this private, preview state until you tell us you're happy with it. Once you approve it, we flip it to public: that's when it gets its real web address, shows up in search results, and anyone can visit it.

## What this is, and what it isn't

This keeps your unfinished site from being seen or found by accident while we're still working on it, and puts the decision to go public in your hands. It is not the same as a bank-vault-grade security system, and we won't oversell it as one. For a small business marketing site, this is the right level of protection for the job: private during the build, public the moment you say go.

If you have any questions about this before you approve launch, ask us. We'd rather explain it twice than have you find out something wasn't clear after the fact.
