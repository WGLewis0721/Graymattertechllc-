The Gray Matter Process

Tech Fit to Completion

You don't have to know exactly what you need. Tell us the problem or opportunity, and we'll help you find the right fit — then take it all the way through to a finished, working solution.

The Roadmap

Ten Stages, One Clear Path

Whether the outcome is a new website, an automated workflow, or ongoing technology support, every Gray Matter engagement follows the same disciplined process.

  1. 1
    Initial Fit

    Start the Conversation

    You explain your business, your goals, what's frustrating you, or an opportunity you want to explore. We ask a few questions to confirm we're a good fit to help. If we're not, we'll tell you.

  2. 2
    Understand

    Understand What's Really Going On

    We take a closer look at your current process, technology, and customer journey, and talk through what result you actually want — not just the tool you assumed you needed.

  3. 3
    Recommend

    Get a Clear Recommendation

    We recommend the service, package, or combination of services that actually fits your situation — explained in plain English, with the reasoning behind it.

  4. 4
    Select

    Choose the Right Fit

    You choose the specific plan, package, or scope from the recommendation — including any add-ons that make sense for where your business is right now.

  5. 5
    Agree

    Confirm Scope and Cost

    We confirm exactly what will be delivered, the timeline, who's responsible for what, and the price — in writing, before any work begins.

  6. 6
    Implement

    We Do the Work

    Gray Matter completes the agreed work, with regular updates along the way so you're never left wondering what's happening.

  7. 7
    Test

    Confirm It Works

    We verify that everything works the way it's supposed to, then walk through it with you so you can see and approve it before it goes live.

  8. 8
    Handoff / Launch

    Put It Into Use

    We put the solution into use and hand it off with whatever documentation, access, or training you need to run it with confidence.

  9. 9
    Support

    Stay on Track

    We follow up after launch and stay available for questions, fixes, or small requests while everything settles into regular use.

  10. 10
    Complete / Continue

    Close Out or Keep Going

    The project wraps up as delivered, or — only where it would genuinely help — we continue as your ongoing technology partner. Never presented as an upsell.

What to Expect

How We'll Work Together

  • Straight answers about whether something is a good fit — even if that means pointing you elsewhere.
  • A written plan before any work begins, so the timeline and price are clear up front.
  • Regular updates while work is happening — no disappearing for weeks at a time.
  • A walkthrough when work is done, not just a link and an invoice.
What We Need From You

Helping Us Move Faster

  • A clear picture of the problem or opportunity you're trying to solve.
  • Access to relevant accounts, systems, or information when it's needed.
  • One point of contact who can answer questions and make decisions.
  • Timely feedback during reviews so timelines stay on track.
Not Sure Where to Start?

Have a Technology Problem But Not Sure What You Need?

That's exactly where the process starts. Tell us about your business, what you're trying to accomplish, what's getting in the way, or ask about a specific service — and we'll figure out the right next step together.
