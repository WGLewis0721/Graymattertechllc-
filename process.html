The Gray Matter Process

From Tech Fit to Finished Solution

Every engagement starts with your problem, not a product. Here's exactly how a project moves from a first conversation to a solution you can rely on — whether that's a business website or a cloud infrastructure build.

The Roadmap

Eight Steps, One Clear Path

Whether the outcome is a booking system, an online store, or a cloud migration, every Gray Matter project follows the same disciplined process.

  1. 1
    Understand

    Learn the Business

    We start by learning about your business, current systems, the problem you're facing, your goals, constraints, users, timeline, and budget — before talking about any specific technology.

  2. 2
    Technology Fit

    Find the Right Approach

    We determine whether the problem actually requires new technology, and if so, identify the type of solution that fits — a website, a booking system, an automation, a cloud build, or something else entirely.

  3. 3
    Scope

    Define the Work

    We define exactly what will be built, the boundaries of the engagement, expected deliverables, timeline, dependencies, and the pricing or engagement model — in writing, before work begins.

  4. 4
    Design & Architecture

    Plan the Solution

    We plan the user experience, workflow, technical architecture, integrations, and infrastructure or implementation approach so the build phase has a clear blueprint to follow.

  5. 5
    Build

    Implement the Solution

    We build the agreed solution — writing code, configuring systems, and standing up infrastructure — with regular updates so you always know where things stand.

  6. 6
    Test & Refine

    Confirm It Works

    We test functionality, responsiveness, workflows, integrations, performance, and relevant security considerations, then refine based on what we find before anything goes live.

  7. 7
    Launch & Deliver

    Ship the Solution

    We deploy, release, and hand over the finished solution — or integrate it into your existing systems — so it's live and working the way it was scoped to.

  8. 8
    Support & Improve

    Keep It Running

    We provide documentation and, where appropriate, maintenance options, ongoing consulting support, or follow-on improvements so the solution keeps working as your business grows.
