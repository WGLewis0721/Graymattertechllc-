Cloud & Technology

Cloud Consulting & 1099 Services

Experienced cloud and IT consulting available for contract engagements, 1099 project work, and advisory roles. Remote or on-site — AWS, Azure, GCP, security, DevOps, and more.

What I Offer

Contract & Advisory Services

Available for short-term contracts, ongoing 1099 engagements, and consulting gigs. I bring senior-level cloud expertise without the overhead of a full-time hire.

Cloud Infrastructure

Cloud architecture design, migration support, and infrastructure management for AWS, Azure, and GCP environments. Whether you need a hands-on contractor or advisory support, I'm available for project-based and ongoing engagements.

  • Cloud architecture & design review
  • Migration planning & execution (lift-and-shift, re-platform)
  • Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation)
  • Cost optimization & right-sizing
  • High availability & disaster recovery planning
  • Available for 1099 / contract work

Cloud Security Consulting

Security assessments, hardening, and compliance consulting for cloud environments. I help organizations identify gaps, implement controls, and build security-first cloud postures — available as a 1099 contractor or advisory consultant.

  • Cloud security posture assessments (CSPM)
  • IAM policy review & least-privilege hardening
  • Compliance gap analysis (SOC 2, NIST, CIS Benchmarks)
  • Threat detection & incident response planning
  • Network security & VPC configuration review
  • Available for 1099 / contract work

Technology Consulting & Support

General technology consulting for businesses navigating cloud adoption, system modernization, or technology strategy decisions. Ideal for small-to-mid-size teams that need senior-level thinking without a full-time hire.

  • Technology roadmap & strategic advisory
  • Vendor evaluation & tool selection
  • System integration & workflow automation
  • Cloud onboarding & team enablement training
  • Infrastructure audit & documentation
  • Available for 1099 / contract work

DevOps & Automation

CI/CD pipeline setup, workflow automation, and DevOps consulting for teams looking to ship faster and more reliably. Available for contract engagements to augment your existing engineering team or lead a specific initiative.

  • CI/CD pipeline design & implementation
  • Container orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Automated testing & deployment strategies
  • Monitoring, alerting & observability setup
  • GitOps & release management workflows
  • Available for 1099 / contract work

How It Works

Flexible 1099 Engagements

Whether you need a short-term specialist or an ongoing contractor, here's what working with me looks like.

1. Discovery Call

We start with a free consultation to understand your project scope, timeline, and technical requirements. No commitment needed.

2. Scoped Proposal

I'll send a clear proposal outlining deliverables, timeline, and rate — hourly or fixed-project. Flexible terms to fit your engagement model.

3. Execution & Delivery

I integrate with your existing team and tools, provide regular updates, and deliver quality work on schedule. Fully remote or on-site as needed.
