Search your business name on your phone right now.

Look at what comes up first, then check four things:

1. Is there a web address, or only a Facebook page?
2. Can a stranger see your services and what they cost?
3. Can they book or ask for a quote without messaging you?
4. Does the phone number match the one on your truck?

If you answered no to any of these, that's your first fix. A simple site can cover all four.
