Friday at 5 p.m., scroll back through your missed calls and unanswered messages.

Take ten minutes and list the last three customer messages that never got a real answer. Include the Facebook or Instagram messages that sat there.

Next to each one, write what the person wanted: a time, a price, or a yes or no. Those three lines show you exactly what's slipping through.

Then pick the one that would cost you the most if it stayed quiet, and decide what the first reply should say. One fix is enough to start.
