The vendor emails new bank details on Friday, with a note asking you to pay today.
Before you pay anything with new bank details, check three things yourself.
1. Call the vendor on a number you already had, not the one in the email.
2. Ask who requested the change, and confirm it with that person by phone.
3. Compare the amount and invoice number with the last bill you paid.
If any of these does not match, stop. Do not reply to the suspicious email to ask about it. A real vendor will not mind the call. A payment scam will push you to hurry, and that pressure is the warning sign.
