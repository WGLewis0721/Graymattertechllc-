The bank statement shows three app charges this month, and you can name only one of them.

Try it right now, from memory: name every app and subscription your business pays for.

Most owners get through the big ones and stall on the rest. The website host, the card reader, and the scheduling app from a free trial that quietly became a paid plan. Some of those subscriptions are for tools nobody has opened in a year.

That isn't a failure. It happens when a business grows one tool at a time, and each decision made sense on its own.

The useful question is simple: which of these do we actually use every week? The answer shows what to keep, what to cancel, and what is missing.
