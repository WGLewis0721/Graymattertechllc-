A client says "I forgot" for the third time this month.

Who was supposed to send the reminder?

If the honest answer is you, you already know the routine. It is a text typed from memory on a Thursday night, after the last cut, to whoever is booked for Saturday.

Miss one name and that slot is gone. Miss it on a busy weekend and it stays gone.

A day-before reminder that goes out on its own does not depend on how tired you are on Thursday. Is that the part of the week you want to keep doing by hand?
