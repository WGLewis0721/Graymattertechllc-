Are you open Saturday? What's the cut time for a fade? Do you take walk-ins? Each answer is correct, and each one costs you a few minutes.

After: a saved draft answers the questions people ask most. You read it, change the one detail that is different today, and send it. It still sounds like you, and you stop retyping the same paragraph every evening.
