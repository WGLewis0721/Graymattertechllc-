The laptop slid off the passenger seat on the way to a job and hit the curb.
The screen cracked and the drive would not start. Every customer address, every photo of finished work and every scanned contract was on it.
Nothing had been copied anywhere else. That evening the owner rebuilt the client list from texts and memory.
A backup is a second copy stored somewhere else, and it only counts after you have restored something from it. Check when your last backup finished, then ask yourself when you last opened a file from it.
