Monday, the new hire calls you four times before the first job starts.

Where is the bleach? Which rooms get the green cloths? Do we vacuum before or after the kitchen?

That was the old way. You trained by walking them through each job, and every answer lived in your head.

Now the same training looks different. There is one page per job type with the steps in order, a checklist for the walk-through, and a note on what to do when a client asks for something unusual.

The new hire reads the page, asks a question or two, and starts. You stop re-explaining the same job every time someone joins the team.
