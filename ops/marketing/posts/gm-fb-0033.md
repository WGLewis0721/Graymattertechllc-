Myth: when the office computer slows down, the business needs new software first.

What's true is that a new tool added to a setup nobody has mapped usually makes the mess harder to see. Before you buy, run a quick software audit: know what you already own and which logins exist.

Then check the basics. Are the files backed up automatically? Does every login ask for a second step? Who do you call when the internet goes out?

Those answers decide what to fix first. Sometimes the first fix is cancelling something you pay for and don't use.
