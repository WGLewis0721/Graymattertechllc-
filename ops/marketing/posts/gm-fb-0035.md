Monday, 8 a.m., a proposal is due, and the notes are still in your head.

Let an AI tool write the first draft: give it your notes, the scope you agreed to, and the tone you want.

Then do the part it can't do for you. Check every number, date and price against your own records. Read any legal wording line by line, or have someone qualified read it before it goes out.

Remove anything you didn't actually promise. A confident-sounding draft can include details that are wrong.

Finally, take out client names, account numbers and personal details before you paste anything in. The outline and the scope are usually all the tool needs.
