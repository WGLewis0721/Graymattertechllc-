Myth: you need a full binder before Saturday closing steps are worth writing down.

What's true is smaller. Start with the one job that goes wrong when you're not in the chair. Maybe it's the color mix, the booking rules for new clients, or how you close the shop on Saturdays.

Write those steps down in plain words, the way you would explain them to a new hire standing next to you. Five short steps beat a binder nobody opens.

Then test the written procedure. Hand the steps to someone on your team and watch where they get stuck. Fix the gap, and keep the page where people can find it.
