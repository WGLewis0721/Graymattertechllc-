Your phone rings at 4:15 while you're under a car.

You can't pick up, and the caller doesn't leave a voicemail. They call the next detailer on the list.

Missed calls like that are the quiet cost of a busy week. Most people calling about detailing want a time and a rough price. Think about what happens to that lead after you miss the call. If the answer is nothing, the job goes to whoever replied first.

A reply that goes out within minutes, even an automatic one that asks for the car and their preferred day, keeps the conversation alive while you finish the job.
