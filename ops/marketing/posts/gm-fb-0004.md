Your phone rings at 4:15 while you're under a car.

You can't pick up, and the caller doesn't leave a voicemail. They call the next detailer on the list.

Here's the honest question: what happens to that lead after you miss the call? If the answer is nothing, the job goes to whoever replied first.

Most people calling about a detail want a time and a rough price. A reply that goes out within minutes, even an automatic one that asks for the car and their preferred day, keeps the conversation alive while you finish the job.

How long does it take you to reply to a message you missed?
