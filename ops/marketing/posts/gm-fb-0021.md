Would your bookkeeper notice if a supplier's bank account changed this week?
These scams often copy a real supplier's email, so the only thing that looks different is where the money goes. By the time someone notices, the payment has already left.
Ask who has the authority to change where bills get paid, and whether that change needs a phone call first. Write the answer down before the next invoice arrives.
