The 9 a.m. meeting starts without the client, again.

Many owners think appointment reminders are a nice extra for a bigger office. A missed hour hurts a two-person practice just as much as a twenty-person one, and a missed hour is often what a reminder would have prevented.

A confirmation sent the day before, with the time and an easy way to reschedule, lets the client answer on their own schedule. Start with the appointments you miss most, then build from there.
