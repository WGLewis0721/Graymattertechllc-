Myth: a stylist who quit in March has no access anymore.

The belief is simple. The facts take a little more work.

Leaving the shop does not close the booking system, the shop email or the shared photo folder. Those accounts stay open until someone turns them off.

Many shops find out when a former team member still gets the client reminders, or when a phone they never returned shows up on a list of signed-in devices.

Make one list of every account the shop uses, and write down who can get in today. When an employee leaves, remove them from every line on that list the same day.
