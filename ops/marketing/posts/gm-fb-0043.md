Wednesday, 9:05 p.m., an office manager emails for a price.

You read it twice from the couch. The price sheet is on the office computer, and you are not driving back to get it tonight.

You reply Thursday at lunch. By then, the office may already have a quote from someone who answered sooner.

Quote requests that arrive after hours need a first reply that does not wait for you. A short message can confirm you got the request, say when the quote will arrive, and ask the questions you need answered. You approve it, and the request lands in one place.
