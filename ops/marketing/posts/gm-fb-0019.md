The invoice looked exactly like the last one from the lumber supplier.
Same logo, same amount, same signature. The only change was a new bank account at the bottom.
The office manager paid it that afternoon. Later that week the supplier called, asking where the money was.
This kind of fake invoice scam works because the email looks normal. Before you pay any changed bank details, call the supplier on a number you already had, not the one in the email. Ask them to confirm the new account.
