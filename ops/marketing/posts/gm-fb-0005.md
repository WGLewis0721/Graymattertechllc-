Before: a homeowner texts on Friday at 6 p.m. asking about a recurring clean.

You see it Saturday, mean to answer, and by Monday they've booked someone who replied first. Their details end up in a notebook you don't open on weekends.

After: the same text gets an instant reply that asks for the home size, the start date, and the best phone number. Those details land in one list. Saturday morning you get a note of what's waiting, and you reply that morning instead of on Monday.

Same customer. Same job. The difference is who takes the first step.
