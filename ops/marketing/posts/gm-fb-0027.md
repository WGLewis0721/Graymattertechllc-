Who else could open your business files tomorrow if you were out sick?

Be honest about it. Most owners think about this only after something breaks. The listing folder, the signed agreements and the client list often sit on one laptop, behind a password only one person knows.

That setup works until the day it doesn't. A flat tire, a hospital visit or a dead phone, and the whole deal stops with you.

A business should keep running on a normal day off. A reachable, backed-up set of files is what makes that possible. Nobody should have to be a hero to keep a closing on schedule.
