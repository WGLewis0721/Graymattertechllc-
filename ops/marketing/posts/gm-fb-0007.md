Saturday, 9:00 a.m., the chair by the window is empty.

Nobody called to cancel. The client just didn't come, and the slot is gone.

You spend the next hour texting people from memory, hoping someone can move up.

Then you remember the reminder you meant to send Friday night. You didn't have time, so it never went out.

A reminder that goes out on its own the day before takes that memory off your plate. Your clients still get the text. You just stop being the one who has to send it.
