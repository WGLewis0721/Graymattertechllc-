Tonight, look at who can still get into your shared email.
Open the admin page for your email account and read the list of users who have access. Remove anyone who no longer works with you, including old assistants and past partners.
Then turn on two-step sign-in for every account that can reset another account. Do the same check for the folder where your listings and contracts live.
If you find an account you do not recognize, change its password before you do anything else.
