Sunday at 8 p.m., open Monday's calendar and count the showings.

Next to each showing, write whether the client has confirmed in writing.

Any showing without a confirmation is the one most likely to leave an empty house and a locked door.

Send those confirmations yourself, in ten minutes, or decide which messages you want going out automatically from now on. Either way, the list is the starting point.
