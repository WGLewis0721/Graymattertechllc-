Who types your customer's address into your system, and when?

If the answer is you, at the end of a long day, the usual mistake is a transposed digit or a missing unit number.

Then the crew drives to the wrong place, or the invoice goes to an old email. The fix is often simple: have the customer enter it once, in the form they already use, and let it flow from there.

Would you want to know how many times it was retyped this month?
