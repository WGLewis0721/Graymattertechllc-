Tuesday morning, the crew pulled up to the wrong address.

Someone typed the customer's street into the system at the end of a long day, and one digit was flipped. The crew lost the morning, and the invoice went to an old email.

Who types your customer's address into your system, and when? If the answer is you, at the end of a long day, the usual mistake is a transposed digit or a missing unit number. The fix is often simple: have the customer enter it once, in the form they already use, and let it flow from there.
