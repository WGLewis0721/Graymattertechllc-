Before: the only copy of every listing photo lived on one desktop in the back office.
When the hard drive failed, the photos were gone, and reshooting them was the only option left.
After: the important files copy automatically to a second location away from the office. Someone tests a restore on a schedule, so the copy is known to work. There is a written order for what to bring back first.
The computer can still fail. Now it is an inconvenience, not a loss.
