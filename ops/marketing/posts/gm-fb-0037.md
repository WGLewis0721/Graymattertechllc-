Thursday, 8:30 a.m., a new client asks for your website.

You have clippers in one hand and a towel on your shoulder, so you send the link to your Facebook page.

They scroll past old cut photos, find no prices and no hours, and text a friend to ask which shop has a booking link.

A barbershop site does not have to be big. It needs one page that shows what you cut, what each cut costs, when you are open, and where to book. Keep those details the same as your Google listing, so a new client sees one answer everywhere.

Start by writing down what that page would say.
