Tonight, pick one file from your backup and try to open it.
Check your backup with something you would hate to lose, like a signed contract or a year of invoices. If it opens, write down where that copy lives and who can reach it.
If it does not open, you just found the problem before you needed the file. Then answer one more question: if this computer disappeared right now, what would you restore first?
If you cannot answer that, write it down this week.
