Ten minutes tonight: put your prices where a stranger can see them.

Many people look up an auto detailer before they call. If your Facebook page is the only thing they find, they have to message you just to learn what a full detail costs. Some will not bother, and they call the next name on the list.

Write down the three things every first-time caller asks: the services you offer, what each one costs, and how to book. Put them on one page you control.

Then add that page's link to your Facebook page and your Google listing. A plain page is enough to start. It does not need to look like a magazine.
