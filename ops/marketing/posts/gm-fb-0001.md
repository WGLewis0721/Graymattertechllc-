Tuesday, 9:40 p.m., a new client searches your shop name on their phone.

The only thing that comes up is your Facebook page, and the last post is from March. No prices. No hours. No way to book.

They close the tab and book the shop two doors down.

You don't need a fancy website for your business. You need one page that answers three questions: what you do, what it costs, and how to book. Put your address and phone number on it too, and make sure they match your Google listing.

If you're not sure what that page should say, start there.
