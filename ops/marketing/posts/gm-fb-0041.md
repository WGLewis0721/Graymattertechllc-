Mid-buff, the phone rings and nobody answers.

Before: your hands are wet with wax, so the missed call goes to voicemail. You call back at 8 p.m., and the customer has already booked the detailer who replied first.

After: the first reply starts without you typing it, and you approve what gets sent. The request lands in one place, and you answer it between jobs while the car is still drying.
