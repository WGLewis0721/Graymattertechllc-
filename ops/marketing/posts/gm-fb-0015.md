Monday's bank alert showed a software charge nobody on your team recognized.
If nobody can say who started it, that charge deserves a closer look. A subscription can start with one person's free trial and never get an owner.
The card keeps paying long after the person who signed up has moved on. The cost is quiet. It is not one big bill, just a line nobody claimed.
Find one recurring charge you cannot explain this week, and ask who started it.
