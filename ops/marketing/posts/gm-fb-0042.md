Four things to check before you retype a full-detail deposit.

Find the last three DMs that said "book me in." Count how many times you copied the same name, car and address.

Look at where the deposit is recorded. If it lives in a text thread, a notebook and a bank app, pick one place.

Compare the phone number in each app. If they do not match, choose the correct one and fix the rest the same day.

Check whether the confirmed time is on the calendar you open on Sunday night, not just the one in your truck.

Any step you repeat every week is the first one worth handing off.
