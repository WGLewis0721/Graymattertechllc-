The truck is parked at the courthouse, and someone asks for the menu.

Plenty of food truck owners believe a social feed is enough. It isn't, quite. A feed buries your menu, your hours, and next week's location under old posts, and a stranger has to scroll to find them.

A single website page with those three things answers the question before anyone asks it.
