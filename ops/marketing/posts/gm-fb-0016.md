Everyone in the shop has read the booking password taped under the register, so it must be fine.
That is the belief. Here is what is true. A shared password cannot be changed without breaking every device that uses it, and you cannot tell who used it on a bad day.
Trusted people still leave. Phones get lost. Passwords end up on sticky notes and in group texts.
Give each person their own login. Turn on two-step sign-in for the booking system and the shop email. When someone leaves, you turn off one account instead of changing everything.
