Before: client alarm codes live in a shared note.

Every cleaner on the crew can open it, and so can an old phone that belonged to someone who quit in the spring. Nobody remembers who was told the codes, and nobody knows whether the note has been copied.

After: each person signs in with their own login, the codes sit in one locked place, and turning off one person takes one step.

Start with the note, then the scheduling app. For every app that holds client keys or codes, ask who can open it right now.
