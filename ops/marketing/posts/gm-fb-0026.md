Before you drive away from the job site tonight, check your phone.

Ask yourself these four questions:

Can you open the current estimate template without going back to the office?

Can your crew see tomorrow's schedule without calling you?

If you were sick tomorrow, could someone else reach the invoices?

Does your login ask for a second step, like a code sent to your phone?

If any answer is no, that's your first project. You don't need a new system to fix it. Pick the one thing that would cost you the most money if it stopped, and make it reachable tonight. Keep a second copy somewhere other than your truck.
