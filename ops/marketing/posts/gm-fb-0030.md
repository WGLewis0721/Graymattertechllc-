Tonight, after the last client leaves, write down the one job only you can do.

Don't pick the biggest job. Pick the one that stops everything when you're away.

Use a plain checklist: name the task, the tool it uses, the login it needs, and who would do it if you couldn't. Describe the way you actually do it, not the way the manual says it should be done.

Tomorrow, put that list somewhere your team can open it. A shared folder or a printed page on the wall both count.

Once that first page exists, the next job is easier to hand off, and the one after that is easier still.
