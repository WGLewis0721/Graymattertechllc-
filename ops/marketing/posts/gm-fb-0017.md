Before: the old cleaner's login still worked the Friday after she quit.
Nobody noticed until a customer asked about a message that had been sent from her account.
After: each cleaner has a personal login. When someone leaves, the owner turns off that one account the same day. Two-step sign-in is on for the shop email, so a stolen password alone is not enough to get in.
The rest of the crew keeps working with the same tools, and nobody else has to change anything.
