Sunday night, a customer emails asking for a quote, and the price sheet is on the shop computer.

The shop computer is locked in a building you left at six. So the reply waits until Monday, and by Monday the customer has booked someone who answered faster.

This is the one-desk problem. The business only moves when you sit in front of one machine. The fix is not a faster computer. It is making your files and email reachable from a phone or laptop, with logins only you and trusted people can use.

Start small. Write down the three documents you reach for most, and make sure they can be opened from anywhere.
