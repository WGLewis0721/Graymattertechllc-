## The problem in the owner's words

"I copy the order from Messenger into my receipt book, then type it again to confirm." "The same customer's phone number is in three places and none of them match." "I retype the address for the crew, and last month I got one digit wrong."

The cost described is time and transposed details. No verified error rate or dollar figure for small business data entry was found in the sources, so posts describe the moment and make no numeric claim.

## What is actually going on

Google's guidance is to use Forms instead of manually entering survey responses in a spreadsheet, because Forms sends responses directly to Sheets and you do not type them in [2].

To send form responses to a spreadsheet, the owner opens the form, goes to Responses, then Summary, clicks More, and selects a destination, either a new or an existing spreadsheet [1].

Google's help says that if you unlink the sheet, new responses are no longer sent to it, but existing data stays in place [1].

Google's help pages recommend a macro for repeated formatting or steps, rather than repeating them by hand [2].
