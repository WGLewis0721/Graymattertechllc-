## The problem in the owner's words

"Saturday, the chair was empty and nobody called to cancel." "I text everyone the night before, from memory, and I still miss two." "A client said they forgot, for the third time this month."

The owner is doing the reminders by hand, and the cost is an empty slot or a scramble to fill it. No verified dollar figure for no-shows in these trades was found, so posts describe the moment and make no numeric claim.

## What is actually going on

A 2013 Cochrane systematic review of eight randomised trials with 6,615 participants found low to moderate quality evidence that mobile phone text reminders increase attendance at healthcare appointments compared with no reminders [1].

In that review, attendance was 67.8% with no reminders and 78.6% with text reminders [1].

The Cochrane review covers healthcare appointments, not barber, detailing, or real estate bookings, so its numbers are evidence about reminders in general, not a forecast for a salon [1].

Under the federal rules, calls made with an automatic telephone dialing system or an artificial or prerecorded voice require the prior express consent of the called party, unless the call is for emergency purposes [2].

For calls that include an advertisement or telemarketing, the same rule requires prior express written consent [2].

## Post angles

- Saturday at 9 a.m., the chair by the window is empty and nobody called to cancel.
- A notebook with names crossed out, and a sticky note on the mirror that says "remind Tuesday."
- Thursday night, you text tomorrow's clients one at a time before you can close up.
- A client says "I forgot" again, and the slot is gone for the day.
- A rebooking goes to the bottom of the list because nobody sent the day-before text.
- A detailer waits in a driveway for a customer who thought the appointment was Friday.

## What Gray Matter does about it

Workflow Automation (services/workflow-automation.html) lists automatic appointment confirmations and reminders among its deliverables. Before automating reminders, confirm with a lawyer which messages are covered by the consent rules and how consent is recorded. The sources here do not settle how a given text message is classified.
