## The problem in the owner's words

"I don't know what technology I need." "I think I'm wasting money on tools I don't use." "Getting quotes for things you're not sure you actually need?" These are the phrasings owners use before they buy anything.

## What is actually going on

CISA's Cyber Essentials guidance says small business leaders should know what is on their network by maintaining inventories of hardware and software assets. [1]
The same guidance recommends backing up critical data and system configurations automatically and continuously. [1]
It also says to require multi-factor authentication, beginning with privileged and remote access accounts, and to enable automatic updates. [1]
Cyber Essentials describes itself as consistent with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. [1]
Ready.gov advises owners to organize a business continuity team and compile a plan to manage a business disruption. [2]

## Post angles

- Trying to name every app and subscription the business pays for, from memory, this month.
- A free trial that quietly became a paid plan that nobody remembers signing up for.
- Gathering the list of devices, accounts and logins before asking anyone for a quote.
- The question of what happens to the business when the internet or the card reader goes down today.
- A myth about buying new software before knowing what you already own.
- Asking which tools the team actually opens every week.
