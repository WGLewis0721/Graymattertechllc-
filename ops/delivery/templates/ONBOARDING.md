## 2. Access handover rules

- **You create every account.** This includes your domain, hosting, email, website platform, payment processors, and any other service. You own them from day one.
- **Invite us, do not hand us passwords.** Add Gray Matter as a delegate, collaborator, or admin using each provider's own invite or delegate feature.
- **Never send passwords by text, email, chat, or shared documents.**
- **If a provider has no invite feature,** tell us before anything is shared. We will agree on a secure method in writing first.
- **Keep recovery control.** Recovery email, recovery phone, and backup codes stay with you.
- **We remove our access** when the project closes, or sooner if you ask. The handoff packet explains how.

## 3. Communication rules

- **Your point of contact:** {{client_contact}}. One person makes decisions and approves feedback.
- **Our contact:** graymattertechllc@gmail.com | (334) 652-2601.
- **Decisions in writing:** Feedback, approvals, and scope changes go by email so there is a clear record.
- **Feedback in rounds:** Send one consolidated list per revision round, within 5 business days of each preview.
- **Regular updates:** We send updates while the work is in progress, so you are not left wondering what is happening.
- **Scope changes:** Anything new is handled as a written change request, with its own price and timeline, before work begins.

## 4. Kickoff agenda

1. Confirm the problem and the outcome, in your words.
2. Walk through what is included and excluded in the agreed scope.
3. Review the client inputs checklist and agree on when each item is due.
4. Agree which accounts you will create and which ones you will invite us to.
5. Agree on the main contact, the feedback process, and the update schedule.
6. Confirm the timeline and the date the clock starts (deposit and materials received).
7. Questions and next steps.
