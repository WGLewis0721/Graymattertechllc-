# Discovery Call Script

*Serves process stages 1 and 2: Start the Conversation, and Understand What's Really Going On.*

Client: {{client_name}}
Contact: {{client_contact}}
Date: {{date}}
Length: 20 minutes

## Opener (about 2 minutes)

"Thanks for making time. Over the next 20 minutes I'd like to understand what's going on in your business, so we can tell you honestly whether we're the right people to help. I'll ask a few questions, then I'll tell you what I think the next step should be. If we're not a fit, I'll say so. Is it OK if I take notes?"

## Questions (about 13 minutes)

1. What problem or opportunity brought you to us? What's not working, in your own words?
2. What does this cost you today, in time, money, lost customers, or stress? How often does it happen?
3. Who is involved day to day? Who is affected when it breaks?
4. Which tools, accounts, and systems are involved now? Who owns each login today?
5. What have you already tried, and what happened?
6. What would be different six months after this is solved?
7. What budget range have you set aside? (It's fine to say you don't know yet.)
8. Who makes the final decision, and who signs off on spending?
9. Is there a deadline or event driving the timing?
10. Are there constraints we should know about: existing contracts, data rules, or a vendor you must keep?
11. What would make this a waste of money for you?

Ask only what you need. Do not promise a price or timeline on the call unless the price book sets it. Items the price book lists as "Price confirmed after a quick fit check" are not priced on the call.

## Red flags

- Wants a guaranteed result, such as a search ranking, a sales number, or "number one."
- Cannot name a decision maker, or the decision maker will not be part of the work.
- Wants work to start before a written scope and price.
- Asks us to take passwords by text or email, or expects us to hold shared logins long term.
- No budget at all, combined with urgent demands.
- Expects to skip the deposit or the written agreement.
- Expects us to produce content, inputs, or feedback they are not able to provide.
- The problem is outside what we do. Say so plainly and point them to a better fit if you know one.

## Next step (about 3 minutes)

Summarize what you heard: "Here's what I heard: [the problem, the cost, the deadline, the budget]. Is that right?"

Then close with one of these:

- **Not a fit:** Say so, thank them for their time, and suggest who or what might fit if you know.
- **Possible fit:** "I'll send you a short written recommendation. If you want to go ahead, we'll agree on the scope, price, and timeline in writing before any work begins."
- **Not sure yet:** "Let's do a short follow-up to check fit. I'll send times that work on my side."

Confirm the date and time of the next step, and who is sending what.
