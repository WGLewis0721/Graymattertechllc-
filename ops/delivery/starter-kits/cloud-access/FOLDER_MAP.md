## Offboarding steps (run in order when someone leaves)

1. Remove the person from every role group in the cloud admin console.
2. Remove the person’s sign-in, or suspend it, and record the date.
3. Check the person’s devices: sign them out of the sync app, or ask the person to return the device.
4. Transfer ownership of any file the person owned to the owner or admin, then record the transfer here.
5. Check that the person can no longer open one shared file.
6. Write the date and the admin’s initials in the log below.
