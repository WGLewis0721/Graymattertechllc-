# Human-in-the-Loop Percentage

Target: 15–25% overall, declining as gates learn.

## Where Human Judgment Is Required

1. Approving situation ideas (highest leverage)
2. Final clip approval (character consistency)
3. Occasional voice/tone overrides when AI drifts

## Involvement by Stage

| Stage                        | Early % | Later % |
|-----------------------------|---------|---------|
| Situation ideas             | 100%    | 10–20% |
| Script drafting             | 0%      | 0%      |
| Video generation            | 0–10%   | 0%      |
| Final review                | 100%    | Spot-check |
| Posting                     | 0%      | 0%      |
| Weekly performance review   | 15 min  | 5–10 min |
| Monthly direction           | 30 min  | 15 min  |

## Timeline

- Weeks 1–2: 20–30 min/week (approve almost everything)
- Weeks 3–4: 10–15 min/week (edge cases only)
- After ~20 validated successes: 5–10 min/week (spot-checks + drift correction)

The system surfaces data-backed ideas. Human only says yes/no or corrects exceptions.
